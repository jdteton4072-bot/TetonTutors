## What we're making

A 60-second launch film for TetonTutors' mentor-matching feature, plus vertical cut-downs
and three key art stills. `[confirmed]`

Finished piece, not a prototype — it fronts the feature launch. `[confirmed]`

## Background / Context

TetonTutors connects students with mentors. Matching was previously manual: students
browsed a list and guessed. The new feature matches on subject, availability, and learning
style. `[confirmed]`

The last feature video was a screen recording with a voiceover; it tested as "clear but
forgettable" and nobody shared it. This one has to be shareable by students themselves.
`[assumed — from a note in the launch thread, not confirmed with the team]`

## Objective / Goal

Get existing students to try the matching flow rather than continuing to browse manually.

Success: 40% of monthly active students complete a match in the first three weeks, and the
film clears 50% average view-through on YouTube. `[assumed]`

## Target Audience

Students aged 16–22, mostly on mobile, mostly at night. They already use the product, so
nothing needs explaining from scratch — the film has to show a *better* way, not a first
way. They're sceptical of anything that sounds like an ad for their own school.
`[confirmed]`

Explicitly not for: parents, school administrators, or prospective mentors. Those are
separate briefs. `[confirmed]`

## Key Message

"The right mentor, first try — not the fourth." `[confirmed]`

## Tone & Personality

Warm, plainspoken, quietly confident, a little funny. `[confirmed]`

Flattering but wrong: **inspirational**. Swelling-strings uplift reads as an advert and
this audience tunes it out instantly. That word becomes the negative prompt. `[confirmed]`

Reference: the unhurried, observational tone of a good documentary short — not a product
demo. `[confirmed]`

## Deliverables & Channels

| Deliverable | Format & spec | Channel | Sound | Ships |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero film | 60s, 16:9, 1920×1080 | YouTube, in-app | On | Week 1 |
| Cut-downs | 15s + 6s, 9:16, 1080×1920 | Reels, Shorts, TikTok | Off by default, captions burned in | Week 1 |
| Key art | 3 stills, 1920×1080 + 1080×1350 | Blog, in-app banner | — | Week 1 |

## Visual & Sonic Direction

- **Palette:** deep navy `#0F172A`, warm amber `#F59E0B`, off-white `#F8FAFC`
- **Era / medium:** contemporary photographic, natural, unstyled
- **Camera & lens:** 35mm and 50mm, mostly handheld, shallow focus
- **Light:** practical sources only — desk lamps, phone screens, late-afternoon windows
- **Typography:** existing product sans; text burned in only on vertical cuts
- **Motion:** unhurried. Long-ish takes, slow pushes, no whip pans, no speed ramps
- **Sound:** sparse piano and room tone, 70–80 BPM. No VO on the hero film; captions carry
  the message on verticals
- **References:** none supplied yet — drop images in `./assets/` and link them here

## Constraints & Non-negotiables

- **Must include:** end card with "Find your mentor" and the wordmark; captions on all verticals
- **Must avoid:** the word "inspirational" in any form; classroom-stock-photo staging;
  claims about grade improvement (unsubstantiated); anyone visibly under 16
- **Brand rules:** palette and wordmark above; wordmark clear space = cap height
- **Practical:** ships week 1 of launch; approver TBD
- **Accessibility:** captions on everything; AA contrast on burned-in text; no strobing
- **Rights:** AI-generated imagery is fine for key art and concept frames. Any recognisable
  face in shipped video needs a release — so the hero film is live action, not generated.
  `[confirmed]`
